About TunSociety

A community platform built around safer conversation.

TunSociety is designed for communities that want readable social tools without losing control over harassment, hate, scams, and repeated abusive behavior.

01

Member-first safety

Messages, posts, and profile updates can be checked before they spread harmful content through the platform.

02

Moderator support

Moderation decisions, warnings, and audit trails help moderators review risk instead of chasing chaos manually.

03

Scam resistance

Scam reporting and policy enforcement help protect users when suspicious behavior starts appearing in community flows.

Why it exists

Most community products either look clean but ignore moderation, or add moderation as an afterthought. TunSociety is trying to make safety part of the actual user experience from the beginning.

What it enforces

Hateful, racist, pornographic, political, threatening, violent, and scam-oriented content can be flagged or removed according to policy and user strike history.
