Platform Features

The tools behind the moderation workflow.

TunSociety combines community publishing, moderation feedback, AI-assisted scoring, and administrative review into one product flow.

01

AI-assisted message scoring

Incoming text can be evaluated for harmful categories before being accepted into the community.

02

Moderation workflows

Warnings, suppression, and freeze logic support a measurable response instead of random manual action.

03

Protected publishing surfaces

Posts, messages, display names, comments, and friend-request notes can all move through moderation checks.

04

Admin audit trails

Moderators and admins can review decisions with saved moderation context instead of guessing what happened.

05

Role-aware dashboards

Members, moderators, and admins land in different parts of the product according to their responsibilities.

06

Scam protection

Suspicious behavior and scam-oriented messaging can be escalated as part of the platform’s safety model.
