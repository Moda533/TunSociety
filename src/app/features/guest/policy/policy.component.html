Community Policy

Clear rules, clear consequences.

The policy is meant to protect members, not decorate the footer. Harmful content categories are blocked, users are warned, and repeated violations lead to automatic account action.

Forbidden categories

Political, pornographic, racist, hateful, threatening, violent, and scam-related content is forbidden.

Protected surfaces

The same rules apply to messages, posts, comments, names, and other user-written text across the platform.

Scam awareness

Users must be protected from fraud attempts, suspicious outreach, and manipulative messaging patterns.

Step 1: Warning

A blocked violation creates a warning with a reason tied to the policy category involved.

Step 2: Suppression

Harmful text is suppressed instead of being published or delivered into the community feed.

Step 3: Freeze

After 3 suppressed violations, the account can be frozen automatically to protect other members.
